Last step of the course: use the whole-diagram idea to find the members that belong to none of the sets. This is the region outside all the circles, and it is the region people forget most often.

The outside region

Everything under discussion is the universal set U. Anything not in the union of the circles is outside:

outside = n(U) − n(A ∪ B ∪ C)

Example

In a survey of 100 people, 82 use at least one of three apps. Then 100 − 82 = 18 people use none of them.

The checkpoint

In a separate survey, n(U) = 60 and n(A ∪ B ∪ C) = 56. Subtract the union from the universal set to find how many members are outside all three sets.

Key idea: “none of the above” is the universal set minus the union.
